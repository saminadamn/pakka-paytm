The mission

Common questions

Isn't this just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot waits for the user to ask, and a language model decides what to say. Pakka watches the journey and steps in on its own when someone is stuck. Rules decide what's wrong, a guard checks the action against an approved list, and the explanation comes from reviewed templates, so nothing the user hears is made up.

Does Pakka decide whether I get the loan?

No. Pakka only helps people finish the application: retaking a photo, converting a file, explaining loan terms, or bringing in a person. None of its nine approved actions approve or reject anything.

What happens to my personal details?

Aadhaar numbers, PAN, phone numbers and email addresses are masked before any text leaves the server, for example when a message is sent to be read aloud. Every intervention is written to an audit log.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Hindi today, written and read aloud. More languages are planned through Sarvam.

What if Pakka can't fix the problem?

On the third attempt, or if an action isn't allowed, Pakka hands the user to a person, who sees the full journey history so the user doesn't have to repeat anything.

How accurate is it?

On our own 500 synthetic journeys detection is 1.00, but those come from the same failure types the rules were written for. On 260 held-out journeys from 13 patterns written afterwards, it scores 0.85 with zero false alarms. Real accuracy can only come from a pilot.

Is this connected to Paytm?

No. This is a hackathon prototype running on synthetic data. It isn't connected to Paytm's systems.

What it can and can't do

We tested the logic. Then we tested it on journeys it had never seen.

Our own scenarios

1.00

Detection accuracy on 500 synthetic journeys, plus 30 of 30 hand-written scenarios. These come from the same failure types the rules were written for, so this shows the rules do what we wrote.

Held-out test

0.85

Accuracy on 260 journeys from 13 patterns written after the detector, with 0 false alarms and 11% of stalls missed. The misses are two failed uploads followed by walking away, and one failed face check followed by a pause.

What it can do

  • Spot six kinds of stall at KYC and the loan offer
  • Explain them in English or Hindi, in writing or out loud
  • Fix them with the user's consent, from an approved list
  • Hand off to a person with the full history

What it can't do yet

  • It hasn't been tested on real Paytm traffic
  • It can't answer free-form questions
  • It doesn't approve or reject loans
  • It only speaks English and Hindi
